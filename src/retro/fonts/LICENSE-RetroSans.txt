Retro Sans (RetroSans.ttf, RetroSansBold.ttf, RetroSansHD*.ttf, the
.ttf.z files made from them, and the bitmaps in src/retro/font.nim)
=======================================================================

Retro Sans is an adaptation of two FontStructions by "lou":

  "MS Sans Serif"       https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384746
  "MS Sans Serif Bold"  https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/1384862

both licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution Share Alike 3.0
licence (CC BY-SA 3.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/),
as converted to WOFF2 and distributed by the 98.css project
(https://github.com/jdan/98.css). Retro Sans is licensed under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 3.0: you may share and adapt it, also commercially, if
you give credit (lou, and this adaptation) and share your adaptations of
the font under the same licence. The licence covers the font only, not
the programs that use it (the rest of Retro Nim UI is MIT licensed).

"MS Sans Serif" is the name of a Microsoft font; Retro Sans is not
affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, and contains no Microsoft font
data. The original FontStructions are recreations by lou.

Changes made by Retro Nim UI (tools/genretro.py):

  - renamed Retro Sans; outlines redrawn from the bricks on a grid of 12
    bricks to the em (units per em 1200, one brick 100 units); line
    metrics of 12 bricks above the baseline and 4 below
  - characters added in the same one-brick style: in the regular weight
    ` | < >, in the bold weight the ASCII characters it lacked (from the
    regular, made bold), and in both the Latin-1 Supplement, Latin
    Extended-A, the euro sign, bullets, ellipsis, single and double
    guillemets, low quotes, daggers, per mille, trade mark, minus,
    primes, arrows, a check mark, triangles, <= >= /= ~~ infinity and
    numero
  - accented letters composed from the base letters and accents drawn
    for them
  - the space widened from 2 bricks to 3
  - "HD" versions (RetroSansHD.ttf, RetroSansHDBold.ttf): the same glyphs
    traced into smooth outlines (one-brick staircases made straight
    diagonals), for large text and high-resolution screens

Retro Sans Arabic (RetroSansArabic.ttf, RetroSansArabicHD.ttf) is made
from the Arabic glyphs of GNU Unifont and is licensed under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 instead (see LICENSE-Unifont.txt). The two licences
cannot be combined in one font file, so the Latin and the Arabic are
separate fonts that Retro Nim UI joins when it draws text.
